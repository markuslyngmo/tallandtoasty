# 10k Run at Bislett Stadion

![on the track](/assets/images/blog/img_1207.jpeg)

I went for a 10k run at Bislett Stadion today. Having such a cool arena right in the neighborhood is incredible. It makes it so easy to head out and get a proper workout in.

I kept a steady pace for most of the laps and felt good enough toward the end to push the tempo and finish strong. Finishing 10 kilometers around the track definitely gives a great boost for the upcoming training.

I am finding this running thing helping me both mentally and physically. It's fun to see progress as well. Can't wait for the next run club.

## Run Stats

- **Distance:** 10.31 km
- **Workout Time:** 56:42
- **Avg Pace:** 5'30"/km
- **Avg Heart Rate:** 158 bpm
- **Avg Power:** 222 W
- **Elevation Gain:** 58 m

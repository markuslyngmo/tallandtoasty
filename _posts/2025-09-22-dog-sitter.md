This week I’ve been looking after Mino, my mother-in-law’s dog. She has quite the story: she was a street dog in Egypt before my husband’s sister rescued her and brought her to Norway. Now she’s part of the family. Watching her nap on the couch and follow me around the kitchen is a good reminder of how much love rescued animals have to give.

Had a session at [Oslo Sauna](https://oslobadstuforening.no/) today. Love being a member. It’s not just the heat and the dips, it’s the people. Feels like a little community.

If you haven’t tried the sauna at Sukkerbiten, do it. Perfect mix of city views, cold dips, and that warm, heavy calm after. I leave with more energy and later I sleep so much better than usual.

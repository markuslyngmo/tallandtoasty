In a few days, I’m getting on a plane to Benalmádena in Spain. A friend has an apartment there, and I’m going down to do absolutely nothing for a week. The plan is: sleep, eat, swim, read, and explore a new city.

I’ve been working a lot this summer, so it’ll be nice to take a real break from the café. It feels both strange and kind of amazing. I’m looking forward to warm evenings, Spanish supermarkets, and not thinking about croissants before 6 AM.

We’ll see if I end up writing anything while I’m there – or if I just disappear into a beach chair with a book or a bit too much beer.

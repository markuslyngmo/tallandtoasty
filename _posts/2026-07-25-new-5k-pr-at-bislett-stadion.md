I headed over to Bislett Stadion for a track workout today. The session felt tough, but it was great to get it done!

I managed to set a new personal record on the 5k today with a time of 24:24! This run is going to be my baseline to build on as I continue training and working on my fitness.

### Run Stats

- **Distance:** 5.12 km
- **Workout Time:** 24:24
- **Avg Pace:** 4'46"/km
- **Avg Heart Rate:** 168 bpm
- **Avg Power:** 258 W
- **Elevation Gain:** 15 m

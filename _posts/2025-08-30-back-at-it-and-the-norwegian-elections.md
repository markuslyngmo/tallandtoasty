Back at the store. The big task now is keeping customers coming back. That means updating the menu, adding new items, and refreshing old favorites. People want variety, so I’m testing seasonal specials and small twists that make the café worth revisiting.

Also: I’ve voted already. Norway’s election is on September 8, with early voting open since July-ish. I made my choice ahead of time.

The election is expected to be tight between Labour on the centre-left and Conservatives/Progress on the centre-right. Main debates are around cost of living and taxes. Jens Stoltenberg, now finance minister, has become a central figure in the campaign.

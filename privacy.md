# 🔒 Privacy

This is a small personal blog, not a business collecting your data. Here's exactly what happens when you visit.

### Hosting

This site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). GitHub may log basic request data (like IP address) for security and operational purposes, as described in [GitHub's own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Visitor statistics, no ads, no tracking cookies

The site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views: which pages get read and where visitors come from. It doesn't use cookies and is designed not to collect personal data; see [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/privacy) for the details. There are no ads, no ad networks, and no Google Analytics. There's no cookie banner because the site itself doesn't set any cookies.

### What this site stores in your browser

A few small, non-tracking things are saved locally in your browser (`localStorage`), and never sent anywhere:

- Your light/dark mode preference
- Whether the click sounds are on or off
- Whether you've already "toasted" a specific blog post (so the button doesn't let you double-count yourself)

### Third-party services

Parts of the site load content from other services. These are not things I control, and they may see your IP address and set their own cookies:

- **Google Fonts**, on every page, for the site's typeface
- **GoatCounter**, on every page, for the visitor statistics described above
- **Strava** activity embeds on some blog posts ([Strava's privacy policy](https://www.strava.com/legal/privacy))
- A small anonymous hit counter for the "Toast this post" button, fetched on posts and on the blog page ([abacus.jasoncameron.dev](https://github.com/JasonRCameron/abacus))
- **OpenStreetMap** map tiles and the Leaflet map library (from unpkg.com), on the [Run Map](/map/)
- **YouTube**, on the [Videos](/videos/) page: the thumbnails are fetched from YouTube's image servers when the page loads, and the video player itself (from youtube-nocookie.com) only loads after you click play

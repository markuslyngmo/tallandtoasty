# 🔒 Privacy

This is a small personal blog, not a business collecting your data. Here's exactly what happens when you visit.

### Hosting

This site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). GitHub may log basic request data (like IP address) for security and operational purposes, as described in [GitHub's own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### No analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies

This site doesn't run Google Analytics, ad networks, or any tracking scripts of its own. There's no cookie banner because there's nothing here for a cookie to track.

### What this site stores in your browser

A couple of small, non-tracking things are saved locally in your browser (`localStorage`), and never sent anywhere:

- Your light/dark mode preference
- Whether you've already "toasted" a specific blog post (so the button doesn't let you double-count yourself)

### Third-party embeds

Some blog posts include embedded content from other services, which load their own scripts and may set their own cookies when they appear on the page:

- **Strava** activity maps ([Strava's privacy policy](https://www.strava.com/legal/privacy))
- A small anonymous hit counter for the "Toast this post" button ([abacus.jasoncameron.dev](https://github.com/JasonRCameron/abacus))
- **Google Fonts** (for the site's typeface)

None of these are things I control, and I don't receive any personal data from them.

# My Gear List

Nobody is asking about my gear list but here it is anyway. This is the shoes, clothes and tech stuff I use every day.

## Running Gear

- **Watch:** Apple Watch Ultra 2   
  Tracks all my 5k PRs at Bislett Stadion and keeps an eye on my pace and heart rate during morning runs. I am in the Apple ecosystem and I am just a causal runner so this works for me.
- **Track & Road Shoes:** [NIKE ZOOM FLY 6](https://www.nike.com/t/zoom-fly-6-mens-road-racing-shoes-wn2ALjkm/FN8454-100)   
  My running shoes I use for longer runs.
- **Daily Trainers:** [Adidas Adizero Boston 13 Boston](https://www.adidas.com/us/adizero-boston-13-shoes/JS4945.html)   
  Super comfortable as everyday shoes, a little uncomfortable when I get to 8km.
- **Running Glasses:** [Oakley Eyewear SUTRO LITE SWEEP](https:https://www.oakley.com/en-us/product/W0OO9465?variant=888392603982)   
  Great running glasses.

## Tech stuff

- **MacBook:** MacBook NEO   
  I run a café and don't do any video or crazy photo editing so this computer is more than enough power for me and it's cute.
- **Earphones:** AirPods Pro 2   
  Awesome headphones. I don't like the huge cups and they have great sound isolation, even on the plane.
- **Phone:** iPhone 16 Pro   
  I used to have the newest phone every year. Listen they are all the same after iPhone 14.
- **Camera:** [KODAK Snapic A1 35mm Film Camera](https://www.kodak.com/en/consumer/product/cameras/film/snapic-a1-film-camera/)   
  I am sick and tired of using my phone as my camera because I never look at the photos anyway(only when I post here on my blog). So I found a really cheap film camera that people say is good. I have just got it and will get back when my film is ready developed.
- **Gaming PC** [Komplett-PC Advanced Gaming a160](https://www.komplett.no/product/1303085?noredirect=true)   
  Phanteks Eclipse G360A Mid Tower (sort), Sapphire PULSE AMD Radeon RX 7700 XT, AMD, Ryzen 5 7600 Tray CPU, Kingston FURY Beast RGB DDR5 5600MHz 16GB, Kingston NV3 SSD 2TB, MSI 27" gamingskjerm MAG 27CQ6F
